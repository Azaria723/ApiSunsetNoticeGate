# Deprecation policy

A public API endpoint may be retired only after at least 90 days of notice. The notice must identify the exact HTTP method and path, name a replacement, disclose known migration limitations, and link an actionable migration guide. The replacement must preserve the old endpoint's essential operation. No emergency exception applies to the demonstration endpoint.
